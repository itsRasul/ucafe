## Evidence and boundaries

The tour uses explicitly labeled illustrative data; the storefront crop comes from the existing product capture. No customer counts, recommendations, testimonials, commercial prices, or entitlements are invented. `subscription-features.ts` and active database plans define the comparison. Consultation retains its transactional Platform CRM integration. New tokens opt in through `.ucafe-ui`; tenant branding remains independent.

## Required states

## Responsive contract

Validate 360, 390, 768, 1440 and 1920 pixels; no page overflow, visible focus, 44-pixel targets, Persian RTL, LTR hostnames/OTP, native FAQ details, mobile plan comparison, and reduced-motion-safe transitions.
