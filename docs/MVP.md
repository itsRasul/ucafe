# Cafexa MVP

## Product promise

Cafexa provisions a premium Persian-first website for an independent coffee shop without deploying a separate application for each tenant.

## Launch scope

- One premium RTL public template
- Tenant provisioning on platform subdomains
- Website content and branding management
- Menu categories and items
- Table reservations
- Customer phone-number OTP authentication
- Owner and platform administration
- Seven-day controlled preview trial
- Prepaid monthly Silver subscription
- Seven-day payment grace period
- Reversible suspension without data deletion

## Pricing defaults

- Silver: 1,900,000 toman per prepaid month
- Golden: 2,900,000 toman per prepaid month, inactive until its features are implemented

Prices are configurable data and payments preserve price snapshots.

## Explicitly postponed

- Ordering and customer-order payments
- Automated subscription billing
- Golden-plan modules
- Custom domains
- Multiple templates
- Multiple-branch user experience
- Advanced analytics
- Page builder
- Custom role editor
